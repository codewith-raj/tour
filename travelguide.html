When to Go

Best Time to Visit Bihar

Bihar's climate varies significantly between seasons — choosing the right time can completely transform your experience.

🌸
Season Months Temperature What to Expect Rating
Winter October – February 8°C – 22°C Cool and dry. Ideal for sightseeing at Bodh Gaya, Nalanda, and Rajgir. Chhath Puja falls in October/November — a spectacular time to visit. ★★★★★ Best
Spring March – April 22°C – 36°C Temperatures rise gradually. Good time to visit wildlife areas before the hot season. Holi adds a burst of colour in March. ★★★★ Good
Summer May – June 36°C – 45°C Extremely hot and dry. Not recommended for outdoor tourism. If visiting, stay in air-conditioned accommodation and avoid midday travel. ★ Avoid if possible
Monsoon July – September 28°C – 35°C Heavy rainfall, lush greenery. Some northern districts are prone to flooding. River landscapes are breathtaking. Valmiki Reserve is typically closed. ★★ Caution advised
Getting There & Around

Transport in Bihar

Bihar is well connected by air, rail, and road — with Patna serving as the main gateway to the state.

✿

✈ By Air

  • Lok Nayak Jayaprakash Airport (Patna) is the primary airport
  • Direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangalore, and Hyderabad
  • Gaya International Airport receives pilgrimage charter flights from Southeast Asia
  • Book in advance during October–February for cheaper fares
  • Taxi services from the airport to the city centre take 20–30 minutes

🚆 By Train

  • Patna Junction is the busiest railway station — well connected nationally
  • Rajdhani and Shatabdi trains run from Delhi and major cities
  • Gaya Junction connects directly to Kolkata and South India
  • Book on IRCTC at least 2–3 weeks ahead for major routes
  • Local trains connect Patna to Rajgir, Nalanda, and Gaya

🚌 By Road

  • NH 19 and NH 28 connect Bihar to neighbouring states
  • Bihar State Road Transport buses cover all major districts
  • Private buses operate from Varanasi, Kolkata, and Jharkhand daily
  • Hired taxis or self-drive cars recommended for visiting multiple sites
  • Road quality has improved significantly on national highways

🛺 Getting Around Locally

  • Auto-rickshaws are the most common short-distance transport
  • Cycle-rickshaws are available in old town areas
  • App-based cabs (Ola, Rapido) available in Patna and Gaya
  • E-rickshaws are eco-friendly and cheap for inner-city travel
  • Renting a private car is best for day trips to multiple sites
Where to Stay

Accommodation Options

Bihar offers a range of stay options from heritage guesthouses to modern hotels — there is something for every budget.

🐟

🏨 Luxury Hotels

  • Maurya Hotel, Patna — flagship five-star with rooftop restaurant
  • Hotel Chanakya, Patna — heritage property with modern amenities
  • Royal Residency, Bodh Gaya — well-appointed and close to the temple
  • Siddharth Vihar Bodhgaya — excellent for pilgrimage groups
  • Expected cost: ₹4,000 – ₹10,000 per night

🏩 Mid-Range Stays

  • Hotel Samrat International, Patna — good value, central location
  • Hotel Sona, Gaya — clean rooms, friendly staff
  • Rajgir Residency — government-run, overlooking the hills
  • International Guest Houses in Bodh Gaya (Thai, Japanese, Tibetan)
  • Expected cost: ₹1,200 – ₹3,500 per night

🏕 Budget Options

  • Dharamshalas (pilgrim rest houses) near Bodh Gaya — very affordable
  • Government tourist bungalows across major destinations
  • Local guesthouses in Nalanda and Rajgir — basic but clean
  • Homestay options in Mithila region for cultural immersion
  • Expected cost: ₹400 – ₹1,100 per night
Smart Spending

Budget Travel Tips for Bihar

Bihar is one of the most affordable travel destinations in India — these tips will help you stretch your budget further.

💰 Food & Dining

  • Eat at local dhabas — litti chokha rarely costs more than ₹60
  • Sattu sherbet and paratha makes an incredibly filling cheap meal
  • Street food markets near railway stations are safe and delicious
  • Avoid hotel restaurants — local joints are better and far cheaper
  • Daily food budget of ₹300–500 is very comfortable

🎫 Entry Fees & Sightseeing

  • Most temples and gurudwaras have no entry fee
  • Nalanda ruins entry: ₹40 (Indian nationals), ₹600 (foreigners)
  • Mahabodhi Temple: free entry, small camera charge applies
  • Rajgir ropeway: approximately ₹120 per person
  • Buy a multi-site ticket if visiting Nalanda and nearby ruins

🚗 Transport Savings

  • Share auto-rickshaws instead of private — cost is a fraction
  • Book train tickets in sleeper class for overnight journeys
  • Hire a local guide-driver who knows multiple destinations
  • Avoid tourist taxis from hotels — negotiate directly at stands
  • Renting a cycle in Bodh Gaya costs around ₹50 per day
Stay Safe

Safety Guidelines for Visitors

Bihar has improved significantly in terms of law, order, and tourist infrastructure — but sensible precautions are always wise.

🛡 General Safety

  • Carry a photocopy of your passport / ID at all times
  • Use registered tourist taxis and pre-booked cabs where possible
  • Avoid displaying expensive jewellery or electronics in public
  • Stay in well-reviewed accommodation with positive recent feedback
  • Keep emergency numbers saved: Police 100, Ambulance 108

🌊 Monsoon Precautions

  • Check flood alerts for North Bihar before visiting July–September
  • Avoid river ghats during high water levels
  • Carry waterproof bags for documents and electronics
  • Some rural roads become inaccessible during heavy rain
  • Keep Bihar SDRF flood helpline saved: 0612-2294204

🏥 Health & Medical

  • Drink only bottled or filtered water throughout your visit
  • Carry oral rehydration sachets (ORS) in summer months
  • Patna Medical College Hospital (PMCH) is the largest state hospital
  • Travel insurance with medical coverage is strongly recommended
  • Carry a basic first-aid kit including antacids and antihistamines
Before You Go

Bihar Travel Checklist

Tick these off before you leave — they will make your trip significantly smoother.

  • Book train or flight tickets in advance
  • Download offline maps of Bihar
  • Carry government-issued photo ID
  • Check weather forecast for your travel dates
  • Arrange accommodation before arriving
  • Carry local currency (cash) — ATMs can be scarce in rural areas
  • Pack light, breathable cotton clothing
  • Bring modest attire for temple and gurudwara visits
  • Purchase a local SIM card (Jio/Airtel) for data
  • Save IRCTC app for real-time train updates
  • Pack basic medicines, sunscreen, and insect repellent
  • Inform someone of your itinerary before travelling solo
