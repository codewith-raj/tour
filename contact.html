Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we get asked most often about visiting Bihar.

🌸

Is Bihar safe for tourists?

Yes — Bihar's tourism infrastructure has improved significantly. Major pilgrim cities like Bodh Gaya and Rajgir are very well managed. Standard travel precautions apply as they would anywhere in India.

What language is spoken in Bihar?

Hindi is the official language. Maithili, Bhojpuri, and Magahi are widely spoken regional languages. English is understood in hotels, airports, and tourist areas.

Do I need a visa for India?

Most foreign nationals require an Indian visa. India's e-Visa scheme covers over 160 countries — apply online at the official Indian e-Visa portal at least 4 days before travel.

What currency is used in Bihar?

Indian Rupee (INR). ATMs are available in cities, but carry adequate cash when visiting rural areas or smaller towns. Credit cards are accepted in major hotels and restaurants.
